## 🔬 About Apadana

**Apadana AI Research Center** is an independent research and software development center focused on applying **Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, Signal Processing, and Advanced Software Engineering** to complex real-world problems.

Apadana brings together two major areas of research and development:

* 🏥 **Medical AI**
* 🛢️ **Petroleum & Energy AI**

Our goal is not simply to build AI models, but to develop **complete, practical, and professional systems** — from data processing and algorithm development to intelligent applications, visualization, reporting, and deployment.

Apadana combines domain knowledge with modern AI technologies to transform complex medical and industrial data into useful software and decision-support tools.

---

The Medical AI department focuses on developing intelligent software for **medical imaging and clinical workflows**.

## 📋 Clinical Standards & Responsible Development

Medical software requires a different level of responsibility from ordinary AI applications.

Our development approach considers relevant **international clinical, technical, and regulatory standards**, depending on the specific application.

These may include:

* FDA guidance and regulatory frameworks for medical AI / SaMD
* BI-RADS and other domain-specific clinical classification systems
* DICOM standards
* PACS integration
* ICD and clinical coding systems
* Clinical reporting standards
* Data privacy and security requirements
* Explainability and traceability
* Human-in-the-loop clinical workflows

Standards and regulatory requirements will be evaluated according to the intended use, target market, clinical workflow, and regulatory classification of each application.

# 🩺 First Ophthalmology Application — Retinopathy

The first application being developed within the ophthalmology dashboard focuses on **retinopathy analysis from retinal fundus images**.

The objective is to create an AI-assisted system capable of analyzing retinal images, identifying relevant pathological findings, estimating disease severity, and presenting the results through an intuitive clinical interface.

### Potential capabilities

* Retinopathy classification and severity assessment
* Retinal lesion detection
* Microaneurysm detection
* Hemorrhage detection
* Exudate detection
* Cotton-wool spot detection
* Neovascularization analysis
* Macular region analysis
* Image-quality assessment
* Explainable AI visualization
* Structured clinical findings
* AI-assisted report generation
* Referral / follow-up assistance

The system will be developed progressively, beginning with the core image-analysis pipeline and expanding toward a more comprehensive ophthalmology workflow.

# 🛢️ Department 2 — Petroleum & Energy AI

## Overview

The Petroleum AI department is built on extensive experience in **petroleum data analysis, well logging, industrial inspection, signal processing, computer vision, and AI software development**.

The focus is not limited to developing individual AI models.

We develop **complete AI pipelines and professional software systems**, starting from raw industrial data and continuing through preprocessing, signal/image analysis, AI inference, visualization, reporting, and deployment.

# 🔍 4. MFL Pipeline Defect Detection — End-to-End AI Pipeline

One of the major petroleum AI areas is **Magnetic Flux Leakage (MFL) pipeline inspection**.

The focus is on developing the **complete AI pipeline from raw inspection data to final defect interpretation**.

### End-to-End Pipeline

**Raw MFL Data**

↓

**Data Loading & Organization**

↓

**Signal Preprocessing**

↓

**Noise Reduction & Filtering**

↓

**Signal / Image Transformation**

↓

**Feature Extraction**

↓

**AI-Based Detection**

↓

**Defect Localization**

↓

**Defect Classification**

↓

**Post-Processing**

↓

**Measurement & Severity Analysis**

↓

**Visualization**

↓

**Automated Reporting**

### AI / Computer Vision Research

Depending on the dataset and problem, different architectures can be evaluated, including:

* YOLO-family detectors
* U-Net architectures
* RCNN-family models
* FCOS
* Segmentation networks
* Classification networks
* Custom deep-learning architectures
* Signal-processing + deep-learning hybrid approaches

The purpose is not to use a particular model simply because it is popular, but to select and develop the architecture that best fits the characteristics of the inspection data.

# 🔄 7. Integrated Petroleum AI Pipelines

A major area of interest for Apadana is connecting different petroleum data sources into unified AI workflows.

For example:

**Well Logs → Processing → AI Interpretation → Geological/Petrophysical Analysis**

or

**MFL Signals → Signal Processing → AI Detection → Defect Measurement → Inspection Report**

or

**Industrial X-Ray → Image Processing → AI Detection → Classification → Automated Report**

or

**Drilling Sensors → Time-Series Processing → Forecasting Model → Prediction Dashboard**

This end-to-end approach allows AI to become part of a complete engineering workflow rather than remaining an isolated research model.
