Apadana AI Research Center is an independent AI research and development company

## 🎯 Our Focus Areas

### 🏥 Medical AI
- Mammography analysis & BI-RADS classification
- Ophthalmology: retinopathy detection & fundus analysis
- Spine MRI segmentation (vertebrae & intervertebral discs)
- Bone age estimation from MRI
- 3D medical imaging & depth estimation
- Vision-Language Models (VLMs) for clinical decision support

### 🛢️ Petroleum AI
- MFL signal analysis for pipeline defect detection
- Seismic data forecasting & interpretation
- Well log conversion (LAS/DLIS → SEG-Y)

- AI-assisted fracture detection

### 👁️ Computer Vision & Research
- Object detection & segmentation (YOLO, UNet, FCOS, RetinaNet)
- Vision-Language Models: MedGEMMA, CLIP, SAM
- 3D reconstruction & depth estimation
- Cross-platform AI software development (C++/Qt, Python)
